# Dev board cost-down candidates (2026-10-14)

Baseline, at JLC prices on 2026-10-14 for a 5-board order (minimum buy and attrition included):

- Parts: **$94.32 per board**.
- **Extended-part fees: 47 unique parts × $3 = $141 per order** (about $28 per board). Two more extended parts are JLC "preferred" (C13533, C7502694) and carry no fee.

The fee is charged once per unique extended part, whatever the quantity. So swapping a cheap extended passive for a basic or preferred one saves $3 per order even when its unit price doesn't change. Prices are JLC unit prices at low volume.

## Status: applied 2026-10-14 (DevBoard README item 37)

All of list A was applied, with these changes from the plan:

- **L201 (A6):** became Changjiang FNR4030S100MT (C167879, $0.047), the same rating as the SWPA4030S, because it has a stock KiCad footprint.
- **L401 (A1):** the SMMS1040 datasheet land (4.1 mm pads, 13.6 overall, 5.4 gap) is KiCad's `L_Changjiang_FXL1040`.
- **L402 (A5):** got its own footprint from the Coilank drawing (`L_Coilank_APS0530`).
- **U203 (A4):** Holtek's basic circuit uses 10 µF in and out. C204 → 10 µF 50 V 1206 (C13585, basic), C205 → 10 µF 25 V 0805 (C15850, basic).
- **Test points (A2):** 13 loops kept, 34 converted. The saving is smaller than estimated, because the old loops were cheaper at the bought quantity than at the qty-1 price.
