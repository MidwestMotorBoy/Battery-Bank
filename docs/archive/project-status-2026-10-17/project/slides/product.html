The product

A 100 W pack light enough to carry on the trail

100 W

Total USB-C PD output, thermally limited; both ports drop to 20 W when hot

82 Wh

Four EVE INR21700-58E cells, 4S1P, in a printed tube

2 × C

Two bidirectional USB-C ports: either one charges the pack, including from a 15 W solar panel

BLE

An iPhone app for live status, temperatures, max charge level and fast-charge mode

2

Two USB-C PD ports with a 20 W guarantee on port 2 when both are in use. Charging is 0.5C by default, with an optional 1C fast mode between 20 and 80 percent.
