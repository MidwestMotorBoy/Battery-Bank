Key decisions

Six changes that shaped rev A7

MCU: STM32WB → CC2340

CC2340R5MODA module, $4.03 against $10.65. TI firmware and toolchain.

4 layers → 6 layers

JLC quoted $88 against $120 for five boards, with free via-in-pad and two solid ground planes.

Discrete charger FETs

Four PGQ04N070 FETs per port instead of half-bridge packages, part of the cost-down.

NTCs → TMP1075 sensors

I2C sensors free the MCU’s pins; their alerts share one IRQ_N line.

Hardware second level

BQ294700 and a chemical fuse added; at first software was the only second layer.

TPD2S300 → TPD4S201

Escapes from its perimeter pads, so no costly 0.15 mm via-in-pad. Adds liquid detection.

Also along the way: fail-safe charge inhibit (the MCU must actively allow charging), one NTC per cell at the gauge, polymer plus MLCC bulk on the chargers, and a 15 A pack fuse sized for 100 W.
