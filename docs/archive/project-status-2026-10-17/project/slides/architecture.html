The dev board

One pack bus, two identical ports

Cells

4 × 21700 holders, 4S, one NTC per cell

Gauge and protection

BQ40Z50-R2, CHG/DSG FETs, 1 mΩ shunt, BQ294700 with chemical fuse

USB-C port 1 and port 2

TPS25751D PD controller, BQ25756 buck-boost charger, TPS62933 5 V, TPD4S201 protection with liquid detection

MCU and supply

CC2340R5MODA (BLE) on I2C to the gauge and PD controllers; TPS62177 3.3 V; INA190 sleep-current meter

6

Each TPS25751 is the I2C master for its own BQ25756; the MCU reaches the chargers through the PD controller’s passthrough commands. Every one of the CC2340’s 10 usable GPIOs is assigned. The board is 196 by 115 mm: holders on the left, gauge column, MCU band along the top, one 36 mm band per port on the right.
