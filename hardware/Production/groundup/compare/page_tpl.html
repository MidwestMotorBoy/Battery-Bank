Step = switch-node swing × coupling capacitance ÷ (coupling + the signal's own capacitance). Coupling capacitance comes from a 3-D field solve of the real copper round each switch node. The signal's own capacitance is its capacitors plus 4 pF per IC pin and 0.1 pF per mm of track.
