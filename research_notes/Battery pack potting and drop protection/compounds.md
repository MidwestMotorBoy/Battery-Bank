# Potting and encapsulation compounds for a 4S1P 21700 pack (82 Wh, about 333 g, 3D-printed shell, tens to low hundreds of units)

Research date 2026-10-18. Every number below comes from the linked source. Where a figure came from a distributor or a third-party mirror rather than the maker's own TDS, that is noted. "Unknown" means the source I could reach did not give the value. Several full TDS PDFs (Elkem, Electrolube, Henkel battery foams) were behind logins or were not readable, so many table cells remain empty. Fill them from the maker before relying on this table.

## Q1. Property table by family and product

### Takeaway
The numbers fall into three density bands. Battery foams (PU and silicone) sit at 0.16 to 0.3 g/cm³ with k of about 0.05 to 0.075 W/m·K. Unfilled silicones and gels sit at about 0.95 to 1.03 g/cm³ with k of about 0.2 to 0.27. Thermally conductive potting and gap fillers sit at 1.6 to 3.1 g/cm³ with k of 0.7 to 3.6. Only a few makers sell kits in small sizes. Most battery-specific foams (H.B. Fuller, Huntsman, Dow EF-6525) are sold in pails, drums and totes for meter-mix machines.

### Cited Findings

**Low-density PU battery foam: H.B. Fuller EV Protect 4006 SFR (A/B)**
- Two-part, flame-retardant, low-density PU foam "designed for potting and encapsulation of battery cell in EV battery modules". Dispensed through a high-speed dynamic mixer head, it self-levels, then foams to about 5× volumetric expansion and cures into a "semi-structural interconnected foam". The maker claims it "will absorb, isolate, and insulate the cells to mitigate thermal propagation". Patent US 11,594,773. — [H.B. Fuller EV Protect 4006 TDS](https://res.cloudinary.com/iwh/image/upload/q_auto,g_center/assets/1/26/HB-Fuller_EVProtect4006_TDS-0626.pdf)
- Datasheet values. Specific gravity A/B is 1.17 / 1.25. Viscosity at 25 °C is 500 / 160 cP. Mix ratio is 100:86 by weight and 100:81 by volume. Working time is 120 s and cream time 180–240 s at 25 °C. Tack-free time is about 60 min. Hardness is Shore A 20–30 at 24 h and 35–45 at 48 h. Free-rise foam density is 0.16–0.19 g/cm³ (10–12 pcf). Thermal conductivity is 0.05 W/m·K. Dielectric strength is 3.0 kV/mm (ASTM D149). The sheet claims "Meets UL94 V0", "No outgassing of Hydrogen gas during curing" and "Up to 5 Times Expansion Rate". The sheet also says "Hand mixing may be difficult", recommends automated dispensing, and says mixing the part A side for 5 minutes before use "is essential to achieve a consistent foam density". — [same TDS](https://res.cloudinary.com/iwh/image/upload/q_auto,g_center/assets/1/26/HB-Fuller_EVProtect4006_TDS-0626.pdf)
- Package sizes: 45.99 lb (20.86 kg) pail, about 500 lb drum and about 3200 lb tote. These are not small kits. — [TEquipment pail listing](https://www.tequipment.net/H.B.-Fuller/10008213/Polyurethane-Adhesives/), [TEquipment drum listing](https://www.tequipment.net/H.B.-Fuller/10007192/Polyurethane-Adhesives/)
- Not found: price, Tg, CTE, elongation and shrinkage.

**Low-density epoxy foam: Permabond ET5442 (provisional TDS, revision 1, 15 Nov 2022)**

Thermal conductivity is 0.3 W/m·K for the foam and >1 W/m·K for the skin. Hardness is 60 Shore D. Lap shear on mild steel is 0.8 MPa (116 psi). ## Q2. Thermal conductivity vs density, and the mass penalty of filling the voids

### Takeaway
At 15 cm³ of fill, conductive gap fillers add about 24–47 g (7–14 % of a 333 g product). Plain silicone adds about 15 g (4.5 %). Battery foams add about 2–5 g (0.7–1.4 %). The full 2×2 bounding-box void is larger than 10–20 cm³, at about 27–29 cm³.

### Cited Findings
- Densities used: EV Protect 4006 at 0.16–0.19 — [TDS](https://res.cloudinary.com/iwh/image/upload/q_auto,g_center/assets/1/26/HB-Fuller_EVProtect4006_TDS-0626.pdf). EF-6525 at 0.3 — [Graco](https://graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/material-profiles/foam/dow-dowsil-ef-6525.html). ET5442 at 0.89 — [Permabond](https://www.permabond.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ET5442_TDS.pdf). SilGel 612 at 0.97 — [Wacker](https://www.wacker.com/h/de-ke/medias/WACKER-SilGel-612-AB-en-2025.03.14.pdf). SYLGARD 184 at 1.03 — [Dow](https://www.dow.com/en-us/pdp.sylgard-184-silicone-elastomer-kit.01064291z). TGF 1000 at 1.6 — [Henkel](https://www.henkel-adhesives.com/uk/en/product/thermal-gap-fillers/bergquist_gap_fillertgf1000.html). 832TC at 1.7 — [MG](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/3/293/1/tds_832tc_2parts.pdf). SC-3000 LD at 2.4 — [Parker](https://phdevv9.parker.com/us/en/product/cooltherm-sc-3000-ld-thermally-conductive-silicone-gap-filler/cooltherm-sc-3000-ld). TGF 3600 at 3.0 — [Conro](https://conro.com/downloads/Manuals/BERGQUIST-TGF-3600-TDS.pdf). SC-320 at 3.1 — [Parker](https://www.parker.com/content/dam/Parker-com/Literature/Assembly---Protection-Solutions-Division/Technical-Datasheets-(TDS)/Datasheet---CoolThermSC-320(Chinese)_DS3724C.pdf).
- Charged EVs (Epic Resins with Electric Goddess) argue that over a thin 2 mm layer the weight difference between foam and solid elastomer is "minimal". They also say foam brings process variability, overflow risk and a need for an outer shell, and that filler in a solid elastomer can be tuned for k and density. — [Charged EVs: Potting vs foam](https://chargedevs.com/battery-pack-engineering/potting-vs-foam-the-battery-pack-safety-advantage/)

### Inferences
These are my own geometry calculations, not sourced. Cells are 70.8 mm long, d = 21.0–21.7 mm.
- The central interstice between four touching cylinders is (1 − π/4)·d² ≈ 0.215·d² per mm of length, giving 6.7–7.2 cm³.
- The whole 2d × 2d bounding box minus the cells is (4 − π)·d², giving 26.8–28.6 cm³. This includes the four edge grooves and four corners against a square wall. A 10–20 cm³ fill therefore matches a shell that follows the cells' outline, or a partial fill.

Fill mass in grams at 10 / 15 / 20 / 27 cm³:

| Material | Density (g/cm³) | 10 cm³ | 15 cm³ | 20 cm³ | 27 cm³ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FlexFoam-iT III | 0.048 | 0.5 | 0.7 | 1.0 | 1.3 |
| EV Protect 4006 | 0.16–0.19 | 1.6–1.9 | 2.4–2.9 | 3.2–3.8 | 4.3–5.1 |
| DOWSIL EF-6525 | 0.3 | 3.0 | 4.5 | 6.0 | 8.1 |
| Permabond ET5442 | 0.89 | 8.9 | 13.3 | 17.8 | 24.0 |
| SilGel 612 | 0.97 | 9.7 | 14.5 | 19.4 | 26.2 |
| SYLGARD 184 | 1.03 | 10.3 | 15.5 | 20.6 | 27.8 |
| TGF 1000 | 1.6 | 16 | 24 | 32 | 43 |
| 832TC | 1.7 | 17 | 25.5 | 34 | 46 |
| SC-3000 LD | 2.4 | 24 | 36 | 48 | 65 |
| TGF 3600 | 3.0 | 30 | 45 | 60 | 81 |
| SC-320 | 3.1 | 31 | 46.5 | 62 | 84 |

- At 15 cm³, a 3 g/cm³ filler adds about 13.5 % to a 333 g product. A 0.3 g/cm³ foam adds about 1.4 %.
- At 82 Wh and 100 W the per-cell heat is modest. A low-k foam (0.05–0.075 W/m·K) acts as a thermal insulator between cells. That is the stated design intent for EV propagation barriers, but it slows heat spreading to the shell. This is a design trade-off to check against the pack's thermal model.

### Gaps
- No source gives measured cell-to-cell propagation results for small 4-cell packs with any of these compounds.
- No data was found on how much these foams cool cells at 100 W in a small pack.

- **Wacker.** Claims CM 185 blocks propagation through the vent and cell-contacting system, and CM 181 isolates cells electrically and thermally and absorbs "vibrations, shocks, and thermal expansion". - **Epic Resins.** Says fillers trigger endothermic reactions that absorb heat and help self-extinguishing. Offers UL94 V-0 systems. — [Charged EVs](https://chargedevs.com/battery-pack-engineering/potting-vs-foam-the-battery-pack-safety-advantage/)
- **Tesla 4680 structural pack.** The cells are bonded in pink PU foam (Munro teardown). The supplier was not identified in any source I found. — [InsideEVs teardown](https://insideevs.com/news/600847/tesla-4680-battery-teardown-under-foam/)
- **Henkel.** No Loctite battery foam was found in searches. Henkel's battery-relevant items found were the Bergquist gap fillers and Stycast 1090 syntactic foam. — [TestEquity Stycast 1090](https://testequity.com/product/10039761-1187951)

### Inferences
- The EV foams are designed for meter-mix machines and are sold in pails and drums. For tens to hundreds of units, Permabond ET5442 (cartridge) or a hobby PU foam are the realistic hand-dispensed foams. The hobby foams have no UL94 or electrical data.

### Gaps
- No independent tests (for example UL 9540A or propagation tests) of the vendors' propagation claims were found.
- Fire-retardant ratings for SHOKLESS and EF-6525 were not found.

## Q4. Reworkability

### Takeaway
There is little sourced data. Wacker markets one silicone foam (ELASTOSIL SC 8xx) as "reworkable". Soft gels (SilGel penetration 150–300) are by nature the easiest to dig out. Rigid epoxy (832TC at 81 Shore D, ET5442 at 60 Shore D) is effectively permanent.

### Cited Findings
- ELASTOSIL SC 8xx silicone foam is offered as a reworkable cured-in-place gasket. — [Wacker 2021](https://www.wacker.com/cms/en-de/about-wacker/wacker-global/china/news-detail-154436.html)
- Hardness figures: SilGel 612 penetration 300 / EH 150 — [Wacker TDS](https://www.wacker.com/h/de-ke/medias/WACKER-SilGel-612-AB-en-2025.03.14.pdf). 832TC at 81 D — [MG TDS](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/3/293/1/tds_832tc_2parts.pdf). ET5442 at 60 D — [Permabond](https://www.permabond.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ET5442_TDS.pdf). EV Protect 4006 at 35–45 Shore A — [H.B. Fuller](https://res.cloudinary.com/iwh/image/upload/q_auto,g_center/assets/1/26/HB-Fuller_EVProtect4006_TDS-0626.pdf). EF-6525 at 30 Shore 00 — [Graco](https://graco.com/us/en/in-plant-manufacturing/material-profiles/foam/dow-dowsil-ef-6525.html).

### Inferences
- Soft silicone foams and gels can be cut or peeled away from cells and BMS boards. Semi-structural PU foam (35–45 Shore A, bonded) needs mechanical removal. Hard epoxy prevents cell replacement and complicates recycling.

### Gaps
- No vendor source quantified rework or recycling, for example with solvents or digestion agents.

## Q5. Adhesion to the materials involved, and cure inhibition

### Takeaway
Cure inhibition is well documented. Platinum (addition-cure) silicones are inhibited by sulfur, amines, urethanes, organotin and plasticizers. Smooth-On names PLA and PETG prints as common causes, and a user report names TPU. Adhesion data for these specific print plastics was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Wacker's SilGel 612 inhibitor list is "sulfur containing materials, plasticizers, urethanes, amine containing materials and organometallic compounds – especially organotin compounds". — [Wacker SilGel 612 TDS](https://www.wacker.com/h/de-ke/medias/WACKER-SilGel-612-AB-en-2025.03.14.pdf)
- Smooth-On lists "Certain 3D-printed resins and PLA/PETG, a very frequent modern cause". It recommends a test patch, a sealed barrier coat (compatible sealer or a thin, fully dried acrylic or shellac layer), and washing and UV post-curing of resin prints. — [Smooth-On support article 46](https://support.smooth-on.com/knowledgebase.php?article=46)
- A Formlabs forum user reports that TPU inhibited a platinum silicone (anecdotal). — [Formlabs forum](https://forum.formlabs.com/t/platinum-cure-silicone-casting-from-a-flexible-3d-print/40458)
- Tin-cure (condensation) silicone generally works in resin-printed molds, but platinum silicones are "much more susceptible". — [Hackaday](https://hackaday.com/tag/cure-inhibition/)
- Wacker CM 185 is "self-adhesive" and condensation-cure, a chemistry not subject to platinum poisoning. — [Wacker 2024](https://wacker.com/cms/media/en/documents/pressrelease-pdf/pm2024/19_battery_show_2024.pdf)
- PU foams in this context bond to the cells and "unitize" the module (EV Protect). Permabond ET5442 gives 0.8 MPa lap shear on steel after cleaning with acetone or IPA. — [H.B. Fuller TDS](https://res.cloudinary.com/iwh/image/upload/q_auto,g_center/assets/1/26/HB-Fuller_EVProtect4006_TDS-0626.pdf), [Permabond TDS](https://www.permabond.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/ET5442_TDS.pdf)

### Inferences
- In this pack, a platinum gel or foam would touch the printed shell, the PVC/PET cell wrap (possibly plasticized), solder-mask FR4 with flux residue, and possibly urethane conformal coat or PU foam. Every one of these is a listed or likely inhibitor. Patch-test on the exact parts, or choose a condensation-cure silicone or a PU or epoxy system.
- Shell temperature limits matter as well. Heat-cure schedules (SilGel 612 test cure of 1 h at 100 °C) would exceed PLA's softening point. That is general knowledge, not from these sources.

### Gaps
- No quantified adhesion data was found for any compound on PLA, PETG, ASA, nylon, TPU, PVC/PET wrap, nickel strip or solder-mask FR4.
- No published Tg or HDT limits were matched against cure exotherm for these shells.
