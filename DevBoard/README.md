# Monolith dev board — KiCad project

First complete schematic draft of the dev board described in `../docs/devboard/circuit-description.md` (draft 5), drawn 2026-10-11 with the KiCad MCP. **Not reviewed, not laid out, not built.**

**Drawing conventions** (cleanup pass, 2026-10-11): signal and power flow left to right, supplies up and ground down; each sheet is split into headed functional blocks with a NOTES block; rails use power symbols (PACK_P, 3V3, GND, and a separate ground-reference symbol for CELL_N, the gauge's VSS); short local connections are real wires, long runs use net labels; unused IC pins are tied with short bars to one symbol; MPNs live in properties, not the value text; every sheet has a filled title block. Readability pass (rev A2, following MacroFab's schematic guide): no text overlaps anywhere, values are the value or part number only (explanations live in each part's hidden `Notes` property and the sheet NOTES), direct same-page connections are wires rather than duplicate labels, sheet-pin names sit inside the sheet boxes, cap banks are at 10.16 mm pitch so every value reads horizontally, and the top sheet carries a revision log.

Wiring pass (rev A3): wires instead of labels wherever parts sit on the same block. Passives are placed on the row of the pin they serve (datasheet style): the BQ40Z50 cell-tap filters are an R/C ladder fanning into the VC pins; BAT/VCC/PBI/PACK/SMBus, the BQ25756 VAC divider, pull-down comb, bootstrap caps, FB divider and DRV_SUP, the TPS25751 I2Cc pull-ups (off a short LDO_3V3 rail), the STM32 BOOT0/NRST/SWD header and the TPS62177 PG pull-up are all drawn with wires. Labels remain for genuinely long runs and cross-sheet nets (I2C buses, gate drive to the FETs, cell taps from the holders, NTC dividers, test points). Local labels went from 275 to 198 and wires from 272 to 350. Nets that are fully wired lost their old names and now carry KiCad's automatic names (for example `Net-(U101-VC1)`).

Tools:
- `tools/setup_board.py` writes the board outline, stack-up, mounting holes (only while the board is empty), JLC design rules, net classes and `MonolithDev.kicad_dru`.
- `tools/netlist_to_board.py net.xml [REF,...]` pushes a `kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml` netlist into the board: pad nets, symbol links, values and fields, and swaps footprints whose schematic footprint changed (listed refs are refreshed from the library). Use it instead of the KiCad MCP's `sync_schematic_to_board`, which mishandles the reused USB port sheet (it left port 1 unconnected and merged the two ports' nets). It doesn't add new footprints; use KiCad's Update PCB from Schematic (F8) for that, then check parity.
- `tools/junctions.py sheet [--drop-labels A,B]` adds missing junction dots (KiCad only joins a wire end to the middle of another wire if a junction is there, and some MCP move/rotate operations delete junctions) and optionally deletes labels by name. Run it on every sheet after any edit, then diff the netlist.
- `tools/remove_items.py sheet spec.txt` deletes specific wires/labels by coordinates.
- `tools/route_labels.py` auto-wires short label-only nets (A* on the 1.27 mm grid, never touching other nets). Its output was only kept where it read cleanly; the main wiring was laid out by hand.
- `tools/check_overlaps.py [-v]` reports every place where text, labels, symbol bodies, pin numbers or wires collide (text widths calibrated against KiCad's renderer). It should print `TOTAL 0`.
- `tools/place_fields.py` re-places Reference/Value text on all sheets into free space next to each part and re-orients labels about their anchors. Run it after moving parts, then run the checker.
- `tools/tidy_fields.py` wrote the title blocks (the older field placement in it is superseded by `place_fields.py`).

Cross-sheet signal nets use global labels (PACK_P, GND, 3V3, SDA, SCL, IRQ1/2, EN_5V1/2, GAUGE_BTP, 3V3_SLEEP). Both ports share one sheet file, so any change to it applies to both ports.

## Board (set up 2026-10-12, not yet placed or routed)

- **196 × 115 mm (planned 175 × 115; see placement), 4 layers, 0.8 mm, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner, ENIG.** The four holders need ~96 × 110 mm, so the spec's 100 × 80 mm grew; holders on the left, electronics on the right (floorplan below). 0.8 mm matches production and the mid-mount USB-C.
- Stack-up (approximate, confirm against JLC's 0.8 mm 4-layer stack at order time): F.Cu 70 µm, prepreg 0.1, In1.Cu (GND) 35 µm, core 0.37, In2.Cu (power) 35 µm, prepreg 0.1, B.Cu 70 µm. No impedance-controlled nets.
- Six M3 board-only mounting holes (H1–H6) at the corners and at x = 106 mm top and bottom, so the heavy holder block is supported at all four corners.
- J401/J501 are placed and locked on the right edge at y = 32 and 68 mm (from the top-left corner); the outline is broken there and each connector footprint draws its own 10.2 × 5.65 mm notch.
- Rules (JLC 4-layer, 2 oz outer): 0.15 mm track/gap, vias ≥ 0.2 mm drill with ≥ 0.45 mm pad (presets 0.25/0.45, 0.3/0.6, 0.4/0.8), component-hole ring ≥ 0.25 mm, copper ≥ 0.25 mm from edges (0.2 mm at the USB-C notches, JLC's floor), legend ≥ 0.8 mm. Custom rules in `MonolithDev.kicad_dru`.
- Net classes: HighCurrent (2.5 mm: pack bus, cells, FET mid-point, VBAT_SYS, PPHV, VBUS, switch nodes, sense-resistor power sides), Power (0.8 mm: 5 V / 3.3 V rails), GND, GateDrive (0.4 mm), Default (0.2 mm).
- Netlist loaded: 320 footprints, `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity` reports 0 parity issues.
- **Placement (first pass, 2026-10-12):** all 326 footprints placed by `tools/place_parts.py`; DRC clean apart from the unrouted connections. Board grew to **196 × 115 mm**: the holder courtyards (94.7 mm) collided with corner holes, so the holder block moved right and the middle holes moved to x = 106 mm. USB notches at y = 32 and 68 mm.
  - Hand-placed: holders, gauge core (IC, shunt by BT101's − contact, protection FETs, fuse, and the VC filter ladder at the VC pins), the sleep-current section, MCU (antenna end at the top edge with a clear zone), connectors, and each charger power train. Port 2 is port 1 shifted 37 mm down.
  - Power train (per port): USB-side half-bridge Q401 with its input bank (4 × 22 µF MLCC + 68 µF polymer) straddling its drain and ground pads, inductor L401 between the two half-bridges, battery-side Q402 with its output bank (3 × 47 µF MLCC + 100 µF polymer), BQ25756 beside the inductor with its gate-drive pins facing the FETs and the bootstrap/REGN caps between them; input sense resistor toward the PD controller, output sense resistor and the 10 A link toward the pack bus.
  - The remaining ~200 small parts are auto-placed next to the pins they connect to (no courtyard overlaps). Worth a review pass in KiCad before routing.
  - Silkscreen: labels on test points (net name centred above every loop), connectors, jumpers and holders only; block titles (MCU, GAUGE, PORT 1, PORT 2, SLEEP CURRENT); "USB 1"/"USB 2" below the connectors.
- **Placement (second pass, 2026-10-12): structured instead of nearest-free-spot.** The ~200 small parts are no longer auto-placed; `place_parts.py` lists every one in `PORT_LAYOUT` / `LAYOUT` as rows and columns, and the auto placer is only a fallback (it places nothing now).
  - Support parts sit in rows on the side of the pin they serve, each row turned the same way (0/180 chosen so the signal pad faces its pin). BQ25756: FB divider, SRP/SRN filter caps and DRV_SUP left; ILIM/ICHG/TS/CE resistors above; ACUV/ACOV divider, FSW and the ACP/ACN filter caps right (C414 was 37 mm from the IC before). TPS25751: supply caps in a column on the left, the ADCIN straps as a 2 × 3 grid (divider top / bottom / 0R) with the EEPROM and its pull-ups above.
  - **Port 2 is an exact copy of port 1**, 37 mm lower (before, only the power train matched).
  - **Hot loops first.** Each half-bridge has its 0603 HF cap (C430, C438) straddling its drain and ground pads (0603 pad pitch = the FET's 1.6 mm drain-to-ground spacing); the 1210 MLCCs sit in one row right behind each FET (three on the input side, two on the output side), with one more MLCC stood on end beside each FET's drain edge, and the polymer bulk cap one row further back (since the polymer change below). The TPS62933 runs in one line, input bulk → 0.1 µF across VIN/GND (pins 3/4) → U404 → BST cap → L402, with the output caps above L402. The VBUS TVS D401 moved beside the CC caps at the connector to make room. L201 now touches the TPS62177.
  - Test points in labelled probe rows: each port has a 2 × 5 field under its power stage (SW1/SW2 straight below their inductor pads, STAT/PG pull-ups under their test points); VBUS and PP5V at the connector edge; pack and system signals in a 2 × 4 field in the gauge column; cell taps CELL_N–CELL3 in a column beside the holder contact each one measures.
  - Board NTCs where the spec puts them (they were all at the MCU): TH301/TH302 beside each port's Q402, TH303 between the USB-C connectors, TH304 in port 1's probe field.
  - Gauge: BAT/PBI/PACK/VCC parts in a row over the top pins, gate series and 10 MΩ resistors either side of each protection FET, SMBus resistors and BTP pull-up beside their pins.
  - Not changed: holders, gauge core, both power trains (apart from the cap banks), connectors, MCU module, sleep-section ICs. Known quirk of the hand placement: Q402's gate pins face away from U403 (the half-bridge package can't be mirrored), so HG2/LG2 need a route round or under Q402.
  - Run `tools/setup_board.py` after `place_parts.py`: KiCad's python save rewrites the project file with default rules.

Planned floorplan (board mm from the top-left corner): holders BT101–BT104 stacked at x ≈ 52, y = 15/43/71/99 (BT101 at the top, all the same way round); headers (SWD, UART, spare) along the top edge from x ≈ 104; STM32WB1MMC at the top-right with its antenna end at the top edge and a copper-free notch past it (ST reference PCB); gauge, protection FETs, fuse, shunt, J101 and EV2400 header in a column at x ≈ 104–135; port 1 and port 2 power stages beside their connectors on the right; the sleep-current section (R_MEAS, INA190s, LDO) along the bottom edge with the SMA connectors at the edge.

## Changes from the circuit description (draft 5)

Found while checking against datasheets:

1. **BQ40Z50-R2 VCC (pin 26)** goes through **100 Ω to the CHG/DSG FET common-drain node** (R106), as in TI's reference schematic (fig. 9-1, R13). The description said VCC needs no external parts.
2. **BQ40Z50-R2 PACK (pin 27)** has an **external 10 kΩ** to PACK_P (R113), as in TI's reference (R15).
3. **TPS70950 output cap is 2.2 µF** (C205), not 1 µF. The TPS709 datasheet asks for ≥ 2.2 µF.
4. **BQ25756 VAC** comes straight from PPHV (before the input sense resistor) with 1 µF. The old netlist's 10 Ω series resistor is gone; that matches TI's typical application.
5. **BQ25756 DRV_SUP** is fed from REGN through 10 Ω (TI typical application). The old netlist tied the two together directly.
6. **BQ25756 sense filters** follow the datasheet pin descriptions on both shunts: 10 Ω in each lead, 0.47 µF differential, 0.1 µF from each side to PGND.
7. **STM32WB1MMC: PB0/PB1/PB2 unused.** ST calls these three pins RF-sensitive and says not to connect them if possible (DS14096 §7). NTC_EN moved from PB0 to PA8, EN_5V1 from PB1 to PA11, EN_5V2 from PB2 to PA12 (design review 2026-10-11), so the 2.7 pF guard caps C305–C307 were removed and PB0–PB2 carry no-connect flags. ANT_IN/ANT_OUT are joined by a 0 Ω resistor (R303) for the internal antenna. NRST has 100 nF.
8. **PP5V bulk:** a 220 µF 16 V polymer cap (C443/C543, ChuangHui SS221M160E7R7R4Z00ZZ, C25503905) was added on each port's PP5V. The TPS25751 table lists 120 µF minimum on PP5V (USB PD cSrcBulkShared), after derating. 2 × 22 µF + 10 µF wasn't enough, and a 100 µF MLCC keeps only about a third of its value at 5 V; polymer caps don't lose capacitance with DC bias.
9. **TPS62177 SLEEP → PB4** (net 3V3_SLEEP). After reset PB4 is the JTAG NJTRST pin with its internal pull-up on (DS13258 table 15 note 8), which beats the TPS62177's weak internal pull-down (1.4 µA at 3.3 V), so the 3.3 V rail boots in normal mode. That is the safe way round (sleep mode only supplies 15 mA). Firmware must make PB4 a push-pull GPIO before using sleep mode, and drive it low rather than leaving it floating. FB is tied to GND, which TI recommends for the fixed-output part.
10. **Status LED on PB5:** 1 kΩ, green 0603, and a 0 Ω (R307) to pull for sleep measurements. Spare header J303 carries PA7, PB8 and, for probing or overriding, NTC_EN, EN_5V1 and EN_5V2.
11. **Gauge VSS = CELL_N** (TI: "IC ground should be connected to the 1N cell tab"). The system GND is on the other side of the 1 mΩ shunt.
12. **TPS25751 capacitors** checked against datasheet table 6.4 (values are after derating): LDO_3V3 (C404/C504) 4.7 µF 0603 → 10 µF 25 V 0805 (≥ 5 µF needed); LDO_1V5 (C405/C505) 4.7 µF → 10 µF (4.5–12 µF); VBUS (C406/C506) 1 µF 35 V 0603 → 4.7 µF 50 V 0805 (1–10 µF, a 1 µF 0603 drops below 1 µF at 20 V); VIN_3V3 (C403/C503) 10 µF is fine.
13. **Fuse F101:** prosemi 1206FA-8A (C21261118), 1206, 8 A fast, 32 V, 300 A interrupt at 24 V. That allows about 6 A continuous after 75 % derating, inside the description's 5–7 A. The 2410 7 A alternative only lists an AC interrupt rating. **D101:** BAT54WS (C7502694, JLC preferred part). **Rev A4 (2026-10-12): changed to prosemi 1206FA-15A (C2838894)**, same footprint and interrupt rating (300 A at 24 Vdc; the pack never exceeds 16.8 V): the 8 A part only allowed about 6 A continuous, i.e. 60–70 W out, short of the 100 W goal (about 10 A from the pack at the 12 V low end). 3.9 mΩ, about 0.4 W at 10 A. Holder-mode current is now limited by firmware and the gauge settings, not the fuse.
14. **INA190s (U201/U202) are hand-soldered**, bought from DigiKey/Mouser (decided 2026-10-11). JLC had only 7 INA190A5 and no INA190A1, and the in-stock look-alikes (INA180/181/185/296) draw tens of µA of input bias, which flows through R_MEAS and swamps µA readings. Both parts are excluded from the position file and have no LCSC number, so JLC leaves the pads empty; MPN and a `Sourcing` note are in their properties.
15. **PPHV bulk above 100 µF is kept.** The TPS25751 table limits sink bulk to 100 µF (USB PD cSnkBulkPd) unless VBUS surge current is limited. The TPS25751D's PPHV sink switch ramps with a slew-rate loop (§6.9: 0.35–0.47 V/ms at setting 0, up to 3.8 V/ms at setting 3), so the BQ25756 input bank (about 125 µF effective at 20 V since rev A5 made C421 100 µF, item 24) charges at about C × SS = 0.06 A (setting 0), 0.5 A worst case at setting 3. The TPS25751 configuration must use PPHV soft-start setting 0 or 1; this is in the usb_port sheet notes.
16. **Charger bulk is polymer plus MLCC, not all MLCC** (2026-10-12). The 1210 X5R banks lost most of their value to DC bias (a 22 µF 35 V keeps about 8.5 µF at 20 V, a 47 µF 25 V about 12 µF at 16.8 V), so meeting the BQ25756's ≥ 80 µF effective per side (datasheet §6.3, TI asks for "a combination of ceramic and bulk capacitors", §7.2.1.2.5–6) took 10 + 7 parts per port. Each bank now has one solid polymer cap for the bulk and keeps only the MLCCs that carry the switching-frequency ripple next to the FETs:
    - USB side (AC_SNS_N): C420/C423/C424/C427 22 µF 35 V 1210 + **C421 68 µF 35 V polymer**, ChuangHui SV680M350E5R8R4Z00ZZ (C49435121), 6.3 × 5.8 mm, 30 mΩ, 2 A at 100 kHz. About 34 + 68 ≈ 100 µF effective at 20 V (≥ 88 µF at −20 % tolerance). 35 V gives margin over ACOV (22.5 V) and the SMAJ22A clamp.
    - Battery side (BAT_SNS_P): C431/C434/C435 47 µF 25 V 1210 + **C432 100 µF 25 V polymer**, JIERR MA25V100M6x6 (C46550465), 6.3 × 6 mm, 30 mΩ, 2.65 A at 100 kHz. About 36 + 100 ≈ 136 µF effective at 16.8 V (≥ 116 µF at −20 %).
    - Removed per port: C422, C425, C426, C428, C429, C433, C436, C437 (16 MLCCs on the board).
    - Ripple: worst-case input RMS is ICHG/2 (§7.2.1.2.5); the MLCCs (a few mΩ each) take nearly all of it at 600 kHz, so the polymer's 2 A rating is not the limit.
    - Leakage: C432 is biased from PACK_P whenever the gauge FETs are on. Solid polymer leakage is specified loosely (typically 0.1–0.2 CV, i.e. up to a few hundred µA, after 2 min); real parts are far lower, but measure the pack drain with and without C432 at bring-up. A hybrid polymer (for example Panasonic ZA/ZC, 0.01 CV) is the fallback if it matters.
    - This is the same structure as the production BOM (`docs/monolith_port1_bom.csv`: 4 MLCC + 68 µF polymer per side). The production polymer is a ≤ 5 mm D-case 25 V part on the 20 V input; 25 V at 20 V is tight for a polymer cap, so 35 V is used here.

17. **Design-review fixes (rev A4, 2026-10-12)** from [`../docs/devboard/schematic-review-2026-10-12.md`](../docs/devboard/schematic-review-2026-10-12.md). Item numbers refer to that review; its "Status" section lists every item.
    - **#1 U203 EN** is no longer tied to LDO_IN (PACK_P would have destroyed it); EN floats on its internal 300 nA pull-up.
    - **#2 ICHG** R413/R513 5.11k → 9.1k 1 % (revised from 17.8k, then 9.09k 0.1 %, the same day): 5.5 A hardware ceiling per port (5.3–5.8 A over K_ICHG and resistor tolerance), so a single port can charge at about 1C. The sum of both ports is kept at 5.6 A by configuration and firmware: the TPS25751 configuration starts each charger at ICHG_REG = 2.8 A, and the MCU raises the only charging port to the full rate and drops it back to 2.8 A on the other port's attach interrupt, before that port's PD contract closes PPHV. EN_CHG_BIT_RESET_BEHAVIOR = 0 and EN_ICHG_PIN = 1 in the configuration, so a watchdog expiry stops charging instead of falling back to the pin limit. Backstops: gauge OCC1 about 6 A, CHG_INH, F101. Set IPRECHG and ITERM by register (the pin only caps them at 20 % / 10 % of 5.5 A).
    - **#5 U101 footprint** is TI's RSM0032A land pattern (1.4 mm thermal pad, one 1.3 mm paste window), `MonolithDev:TI_RSM0032A_...`; KiCad's generic part had a 2.8 mm pad.
    - **#6 D403/D503** ESD2CAN24 → PESD5V0S2BT (5 V, C49338, same SOT-23 pinout): D+/D− reach TPS25751 GPIO4/5, rated 6 V. CC keeps the 24 V part.
    - **#7** R101–R104 → 1206; **J101 is DNP**; F101 was DNP (hand-fit after checking the cells) until rev A5, item 25. The cell-tap ladder is at 2.5 mm pitch.
    - **#8 / m1** DNP footprints on Q102: C109 gate-to-GND (inrush), D102 across R111 (fast turn-off), D103 15 V gate–source clamp.
    - **#9** SMBus pull-ups R304/R305 now hang off SMB_PU, fed from 3V3 by R312 (0R). Remove R312 and supply TP308 for EV2400 work while 3V3 is down.
    - **#10** BTP_INT → Q103 2N7002 (R117 470k gate pull-down) → GAUGE_BTP: PA6 never sees the gauge pin's unbounded high level. GAUGE_BTP is now **active low**.
    - **#11** U301 MPN STM32WB1MMCH6TR / C5456183 (LCSC had none in stock on 2026-10-12: check JLC or consign).
    - **Minor:** C105 2.2 µF 50 V 0805; C205 4.7 µF 25 V 0805; C409/C509 1 µF 50 V 0805; R433/R533 107k (PP5V 5.08 V) with a DNP 10 pF feed-forward cap C447/C547 across each; R431/R531 2512; R204/R205 49.9 Ω fitted; vertical SMAs (Amphenol 132134) for J201/J202; U204 NC pin to GND; IRQ1/IRQ2 100k pull-ups (R314/R315); 1k series resistors R316–R318 between the MCU and J303; reset button SW301; BOOT0 pad TP306; 3V3_SLEEP pad TP307 with a DNP 100k pull-up (R313); keyed SWD header (Samtec FTSH-105-01-L-DV-K, SMD footprint); "not GND" silk under TP102 and TX/RX/G over J302; MPN/LCSC/voltage ratings filled for the parts the review listed.
    - **Wake + bench supply:** SW101 (CELL_P → 4.7k → PACK_P) wakes the gauge from SHUTDOWN without USB; J104 feeds PACK_P from a bench supply.
    - **Not changed:** D401/D501 stay SMAJ22A (meets TPS25751 footnote 4; TVS2200 has no LCSC/JLC stock). R430/R530 stay copper net ties. FSW stays 600 kHz (production copy). No second-level protector on rev 1.

18. **Open-question decisions (rev A4, 2026-10-12)**, see the review doc's "Decisions on the open questions":
    - **CHG_INH:** MCU PB8 → R435/R535 (1 k) → each BQ25756 CE. High stops charging whatever the TPS25751 does. (Rev A5 reversed the default, item 23: R417/R517 now pull CE up to 3V3, so the MCU must drive CHG_INH low to allow charging.) Firmware asserts it when the gauge is silent, misconfigured or implausible, or a protection FET is stuck on. J303 pin 6 reaches it through R319. This replaces the spare PB8.
    - **J103** is 4-pin in the EV2400 SMBus order: 1 VSS, 2 SCL, 3 SDA, 4 not used.
    - **"FIT CELLS BEFORE USB"** on the silkscreen under the holders; with no cells the charger holds PACK_P at about 16.8 V.
    - Gauge configuration: 4S, FET_EN, precharge off (no external precharge FET), short OCD/OCC recovery delays.

19. **Precision resistors rounded to stocked 1 % parts (2026-10-12).** Only the two feedback dividers that set a voltage stay 0.1 %: R418/R419 (charge voltage, needs about ±1 %) and R433/R434 (PP5V, margin to the TPS25751's 4.9 V minimum). The rest move to UNI-ROYAL 1 % (all in stock at LCSC): R413 9.1k (ICHG 5.5 A), R414 3.6k (ILIM 5.6 A), R415/R416 1.8k/5.6k (TS network: hot 94.5 °C, warm 74 °C, cool 18.8 °C, cold −7 °C; an open NTC still reads as cold in charge mode, with more margin than before), R412/R410 40.2k, R409 137k (now 0603: no 0805 stock), R411 and R308–R311 10k. Unique 0.1 % parts drop from 10 to 4.

20. **BQ25756 bootstrap diodes added (2026-10-12).** TI's BQ25756 schematic checklist lists D2/D3, "BTST1/BTST2 diode-OR", as **required** Schottky diodes from DRV_SUP to each BTST node (the datasheet pin table only mentions the capacitor, and TI's typical application draws them). Each port now has D404/D405 (D504/D505): LRB521S-40T1G (LRC, C135802), 40 V 200 mA Schottky, SOD-523, anode on a new DRV_SUP label, cathode on the BTST cap. Placed in line with the BTST caps (D405 under C411 left of C413, D404 right of C412), clear of the SW1/SW2 copper. The production BOM (docs/monolith_port1_bom.csv) was missing them too and now lists D3/D4.

21. **One NTC per cell (2026-10-12, rev 2 item 1 pulled into rev 1).** Gauge TS2–TS4 are no longer tied to VSS: J105–J107 (2-pin, like J102) take a wired 10k 103AT NTC each, taped to BT102–BT104. All four headers sit beside the tap test point of the cell they watch (J102 moved from the gauge column to beside TP102). The welded pack's thermistors use the same headers. Keep the sensors insulated: each can is at its cell's negative terminal (up to about 12.6 V above VSS), the TS pins are rated 2.1 V. Enable TS2–TS4 in gauge data flash.

22. **ESD caps across the protection FETs (2026-10-12).** TI's BQ40Z50-R2 reference has two series pairs: C1/C2 (0.1 µF + 0.1 µF) across the back-to-back FET pair, cell side to pack side (sec. 9.2.2.1.1, fig. 9-2), and a second pair across PACK+/PACK− (sec. 9.2.2.1.5). Rev 1 only had the PACK pair (C107/C108, PACK_P to GND, unchanged). Added C110/C111 (0.1 µF 50 V 0603, C14663) from CELL_P to PACK_P, placed in the gap between Q101 and Q102; route them short and wide.

23. **CHG_INH fails safe, plus a watchdog (rev A5, 2026-10-13; review 2 decisions D1–D3).** R417/R517 changed from a 10k pull-down to a **47k pull-up to 3V3** (C25819). The MCU drives CHG_INH (PB8) **low** to allow charging; high, hi-Z, reset, a hang or an unprogrammed MCU all stop charging at CE. A dead pack still recovers from USB: with 3V3 down, CE reads low. Levels: MCU low gives CE = 3.3 V × 1k/48k = 0.07 V (VIL max 0.4 V); hi-Z gives about 3.25 V (VIH min 1.3 V). A 10k pull-up would only reach 0.30 V low. Charging needs the MCU in Run or Stop0–2 (GPIO state held), never Standby. PB8 low costs 138 µA (two 47k pull-ups), so it is low only while charging and high in idle. The full pin-by-state plan, reconciled with the 150 µA sleep budget, is [`../docs/devboard/mcu-io-power-states.md`](../docs/devboard/mcu-io-power-states.md). A hang is cleared by the STM32 IWDG in hardware mode (option byte IWDG_SW = 0): it starts at every reset, firmware cannot stop it, and it runs from the LSI clock. During the reset CE goes high, so charging pauses until firmware allows it again. Bring-up consequence: flash the MCU before testing USB charging. Other decisions: ICHG stays 9.1k (5.5 A, D2); F101 stays 15 A on the condition that the gauge cut-off under load is 2.8 V/cell or higher (D3).

24. **Review 2 follow-ups (rev A5, 2026-10-13).**
    - **BAT fed from the cell top, as TI fig. 9-12 (D1 on 4P):** D101 anode moved from CELL_P (after F101) to CELL_P_RAW, the VC4 Kelvin node. The gauge stays powered and logging if F101 opens or before it is fitted. Consequence: a meter across the F101 pads no longer sees the gauge's own supply current.
    - **USB-side bulk C421/C521: 68 µF → 100 µF 35 V polymer** (Honor Elec HV1V107M0607PZ, C5246554). Same 6.3 mm footprint, 7.7 mm tall. About 99 µF worst case against the BQ25756's 80 µF minimum after derating (was 72–75 µF). Datasheet: ESR 48 mΩ, ripple 1.5 A rms. Worst-case bank ripple is about 5 A rms (reverse mode, 10 V pack boosted to 20 V at 5 A); the four 22 µF MLCCs (about 11 mΩ at 600 kHz) take about 80 %, so the polymer sees about 1 A rms (about 50 mW).
    - **Bootstrap diodes → LRB520S-40T1G** (C132124, same SOD-523): 9–20× lower reverse leakage.
    - **Q103 → BSS138** (onsemi, C52895): Vgs(th) max 1.5 V against about 2.6 V guaranteed BTP_INT drive.
    - **C109 (DNP) → 4.7 nF:** 0.1 µF would slow Q102's short-circuit turn-off past its SOA. Its note says to fit D103 whenever C109 is fitted.
    - **New HF caps:** C449/C549 0.1 µF on each TPS25751 PPHV; C450/C550 10 nF on VBUS by the receptacle (TI layout example).
    - **New test points:**
        - GND scope-ground loops: TP412/TP512 beside each port's SW1/SW2 probes (TH304 moved 1 mm left), and TP109/TP309 in a third row of the pack probe field.
        - TP108 on CELL_P_RAW, beside F101.
        - Small pads: TP413/TP513 CE_N (beside R435), TP414/TP514 LDO_3V3, TP206 LDO_IN, TP207 5V_AMP.
    - **R306 1k → 330 Ω:** about 1 mA in the InGaN status LED instead of 0.5 mA.
    - **C201** is now flagged DNP (its value already said so).
    - **JLC numbers on every resistor and capacitor:** UNI-ROYAL and Samsung/FH basic parts where they exist. Values whose rating changed to match the stocked part:
        - 0.1 µF 25 V → 50 V;
        - C407 10 µF 10 V → 25 V;
        - C207 22 µF 10 V → 25 V;
        - C410/C411 4.7 µF 16 V → 25 V;
        - C441/C442 22 µF on PP5V, 35 V → 25 V (5 V net).

25. **F101 is JLC-placed again (rev A5, 2026-10-13).** Hand-fitting it meant soldering next to an installed 16.8 V stack with no fuse, and since item 24 (BAT fed before F101) leaving it off no longer disconnected the gauge either. The loads were never at risk from it: everything is on PACK_P behind Q101/Q102, which stay off on a fresh gauge (FET_EN off, Mfg Status Init 0x0000). A reversed or missing cell finds no path (the DSG FET body diode blocks discharge, the CHG FET body diode blocks charge). **Procedure:**
    1. Insert the cells.
    2. Check each cell at TP102–TP105 and the stack at TP108.
    3. Only then wake the gauge (SW101, J104 or USB) and configure it (precharge thresholds, then FET_EN).

    Given up: measuring total pack drain across the F101 pads. Use R_MEAS for the 3V3 branch and the gauge's current reading for the rest.

26. **SMD test loops instead of Keystone through-hole (rev A5, 2026-10-13).** All 45 loop test points are now ronghe RH-S2761-46R (LCSC C55121754, about $0.12–0.20 each, about 10k in stock): an SMT loop 1.60 × 0.80 × 1.15 mm on an 0603 land pattern, the JLC-stocked equivalent of the Harwin S2761-46R (3.5 A). Footprint `MonolithDev:TestPoint_Harwin_S2761_0603` (tools/make_footprints.py section 11): two 0603 pads, both pin 1 and marked jumpered, since the loop joins them. JLC places them with the rest; no through-hole assembly. They are small: fine for hook probes and mini-grabbers, but too small for a scope's alligator ground clip, so use a ground spring or a short ground lead on the GND loops. The bare 1.5 mm probe pads (TP306, TP307, TP413/TP513, TP414/TP514, TP206, TP207) stay pads.

27. **Open-item pass (rev A5, 2026-10-13).**
    - **D104 SS34** (C8678) in series with the J104 bench input: blocks a reversed lead, and keeps a live PACK_P off the bare header pins.
    - **D401** is drawn with a zener-style (unidirectional) symbol, cathode on VBUS.
    - **Headers have JLC numbers** (XFCN PZ254V-11 family, like J102): J103 4P C2691448; J302 and J402/J502 3P C2937625; J303 7P C492406; JP201 2P C492401.
    - **Shunts:** JP201/JP202 notes say each needs a 2.54 mm shunt (hand).
    - **J301** notes now say keyed shroud, all pins fitted (not "pin 7 removed").
    - **Sheet text:** port sheet "5.08 V" and "ILIM 5.6 A".
    - **Thermistor notes:** state Murata B25/50 = 3380 K / B25/85 = 3435 K; use the R-T table.
    - **Gauge note 4:** insulated cell NTCs only.
    - **Antenna keep-out rule area** on the board.
    - **Bring-up checklist:** [`../docs/devboard/bringup-config-checklist.md`](../docs/devboard/bringup-config-checklist.md) gathers every configuration item, with review 2's precharge correction.
